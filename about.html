Who We Are

Hanan Academy is a digital learning platform dedicated to helping students and professionals in Somalia and East Africa develop practical digital skills that matter in today's world.

We believe that quality education should be accessible to everyone. Our courses are designed to be simple, practical, and directly connected to real career opportunities.

Founded with the goal of bridging the digital skills gap, we offer training in web development, graphic design, digital marketing, and much more.

Explore Our Courses

🎯 Our Mission

"Empowering the next generation with practical digital skills."

We are committed to delivering high-quality, affordable digital education that creates real opportunities for students and professionals across East Africa.

🌍 Our Vision

Become one of the leading digital academies in Somalia and East Africa.

We envision a future where every young person in our region has the digital skills they need to thrive in the modern economy.
